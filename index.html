The exercises are designed to surface blind spots, challenge inherited patterns, and build a complete decision-making framework. Free AI gives you answers. This system gives you a method you own forever.
Wouldn't working with a real person be better?
Human coaches bring their own biases, interpretations, and agendas—often unconsciously. They may push their vision of what your life "should" look like. This system has no agenda except clarity. It doesn't tell you what to choose. It gives you the framework to choose consciously.
