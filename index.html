The Pattern Recognition Prompt

I need help seeing a pattern I'm too close to recognize. I'm going to describe my last 2-3 significant relationships. For each one, I'll share: - How we met and what initially attracted me - The relationship dynamic (who pursued whom, who compromised more, what the "tone" of the relationship was) - How it ended (or why it's stuck) - What I told myself about why I chose this person After I share this, please help me see: 1. What keeps repeating across these relationships that I might be calling "chemistry" or "connection"? 2. Where do I abandon my own requirements? What pattern of self-betrayal shows up? 3. If my body could speak about these relationships (tightness, openness, anxiety, calm)—what has it been trying to tell me? 4. Based on these patterns, what would a structurally different choice look like for me? Not "the opposite," but what selection criteria would I need to calibrate? Please be kind but honest. I'm ready to see what I haven't been able to see alone. --- [Paste your relationship history here. Be specific about what attracted you, how the dynamic played out, and how you felt in your body throughout each relationship.]
